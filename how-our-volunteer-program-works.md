We hear the same question a lot: "I would like to volunteer, but I have never taught before — is that a problem?" It is not. Most of our educators are not professional teachers; they are fluent or native speakers who care about making language access more equal. Here is exactly how the process works.

## Step One: Apply

The application takes about ten minutes. We ask about your language fluency, availability, and why you are interested in volunteering. There is no minimum commitment beyond roughly one hour a week, so it fits around a full-time job or studies.

## Step Two: A Short Interview and Language Check

Every applicant has a brief video call with our coordination team. We confirm fluency, answer questions, and get a sense of your teaching style and availability. This is not a formal exam — it is a conversation, usually 15 to 20 minutes.

## Step Three: Free Onboarding

Before you are matched with a student, you will complete a short, free onboarding session covering our lesson structure, how to use our scheduling tools, and tips for teaching adult learners with varying levels of English, French, or German.

## Step Four: Getting Matched

Once onboarded, we match you with a student based on your language, availability, and teaching preferences. You will receive a short profile with their current level and goals before your first lesson, so you can plan accordingly.

## Step Five: Teaching, With Support

Your coordinator stays available throughout — for scheduling questions, lesson planning help, or just a check-in on how things are going. Most volunteers describe the ongoing support as one of the things that makes the experience sustainable, even alongside a busy life.

If an hour a week sounds like something you could commit to, we would love to have you. Visit our Get Involved page to start your application.
