For many students, fluency in English, French, or German is not a hobby — it is a practical necessity that shapes access to university scholarships, remote work, and communication with the wider world. Yet quality language instruction is often out of reach for students facing disrupted schooling, limited resources, or restricted movement.

## Education Does Not Pause Evenly

School closures, infrastructure damage, and displacement disrupt formal education unevenly, and language classes are often among the first casualties. A student who might otherwise be preparing for a language proficiency exam can lose months or years of consistent instruction through no fault of their own.

## Language Skills Open Doors

English proficiency is frequently a requirement for international scholarships and remote job applications. French and German open pathways to opportunities in additional countries and industries. For a generation of Palestinian students navigating an uncertain economy, these language skills can be the difference between an application considered and one set aside.

## Why We Focus on Volunteer Educators

Hiring professional tutors at scale is expensive, and grants alone cannot cover the ongoing cost of one-on-one instruction for thousands of students. By building a network of volunteer educators, we can offer free, personalised lessons that would otherwise cost families money they do not have — while giving volunteers worldwide a direct, tangible way to help.

## What "Personalised" Actually Means

Every student is matched based on their current level, goals, and schedule rather than placed into a generic class. A student preparing for a university interview gets different lessons than one starting from the alphabet. This individual attention, delivered consistently over months, is what turns a few phrases into real fluency.

## Every Volunteer Hour Counts

An hour of your time each week might not feel like much, but for a student who has been unable to access consistent instruction, it can be transformative. That is the whole premise behind Ramzunā Languages: connect people who have language skills to teach with people who need the chance to learn them, and remove cost as a barrier entirely.
