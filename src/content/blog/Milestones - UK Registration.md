## A New Chapter

We are delighted to announce a new chapter for Ramzunā. We are officially registered in the UK and this registration now allows us to apply for grants, secure partnerships and become more sustainable.

A special thanks goes to Juliette D for building Ramzunā from the ground up and fostering its growth. We also want to thank our incredible volunteer teachers who make this work possible and have made a profound impact on our students.  We are incredibly grateful for our students’ trust and support. Finally, we extend our thanks to our wider community and everyone who believed in us.
