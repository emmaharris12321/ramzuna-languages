## Four Children were killed with their mother

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Four children, aged four, five and eight, were tragically killed with their mother in Deir al-Balah due to heavy rainfall.  This catastrophe occurs amidst the ongoing displacement crisis, with over 1.7 million Palestinians still living in tents, damaged buildings or shelters vulnerable to flooding, cold and collapse.
</p>

## 70,000 tents flooded after heavy rains

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Around 70,000 tents providing shelter for an estimated 350,000 Palestinians including 210,000 children, were flooded across Gaza. The heavy rainfall caused damage and destruction to shelters latrines and belongings, displacing tens of thousands of families.
</p>

## Many Palestinians reside in flood-prone areas

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Furthermore, 950,000 displaced Palestinians reside in flood-prone areas.  Without adequate shelter working water and sanitation systems, tents, and essential restoration equipment, the risk of further loss of life, particularly among children, remains high.  This is especially concerning given the already tragic loss of 11 children last winter due to cold. This exacerbates the suffering of the civilian population still facing military attacks despite a year-old ceasefire. 
</p>
