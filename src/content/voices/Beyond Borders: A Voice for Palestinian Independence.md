<div class="poem-block">
<p>Dear world,</p>
</div>
<div class="poem-block">
<p>Have you ever imagined what it feels like to love a homeland with all your heart,yet still dream of the simple freedom to live in it?</p>
<p>To wish for a future where children can grow up without fear,families can live in peace, and people can wake up each morning knowing that their lives and futures are in their own hands?</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I write these words as a Palestinian girl from Gaza,not simply to speak about a national occasion,but to share what independence means to someone who has grown up longing for it.</p> 
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>To me, independence is more than a political declaration or a flag.</p> 
<p>It is the freedom to call a place home without fear,to walk its streets in peace, to dream without limitations,and to build a future on the land we belong to.</p> 
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>It is the right to live an ordinary life,with all the little moments that many people around the world may take for granted.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>And perhaps this is what I want the world to understand:behind every flag is a human story, behind every homeland is a heart that belongs to it,and behind every call for independence are people who simply wish to live with dignity, freedom, and hope.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>We do not ask the world to feel sorry for us; we ask it to understand that freedom, justice, and self-determination are not privileges reserved for some nations.</p> 
<p>They are rights that belong to all people.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>On this day, my wish is simple: that one day, Palestinian children will speak about independence not as a distant dream,but as a reality they have the chance to live.</p> 
<p>Because no one should have to spend a lifetime wishing for the freedom to simply call their homeland home.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>To every heart around the world: may you always know the value of freedom,and may Palestine one day know it in every sense of the word. 🇵🇸</p>
</div>
