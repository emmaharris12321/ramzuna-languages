<div class="poem-block">
<p>Then, some women caught my attention. They were carrying wood on their backs. Their faces seemed very red in the cold morning. At that moment, I realised that every group suffers most in war. I wished I could help them all.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I made a promise to myself: once I can work, I will come back to this place, to these people who are often overlooked or considered, and I will help them.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I took a breath and waited for a suitable car, avoiding crowded transportation to feel safe.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>After five more minutes, we finally got a good car to ride.</p>
</div>

<h2>A New Adventure Started</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Abood opened the car door; we entered and sat down. Once seated, I got ready for the ride&rsquo;s delights.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I recalled going to an entertainment venue. I closed my eyes, cleared everything from my mind, took a breath, relaxed, and opened my eyes again.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>The car continued along a proper road for cars. People waited patiently for their turn to walk. There were many means of transportation.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Suddenly, I realised that we would be stuck in a traffic jam within minutes.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>This meant that my ride would stop. I never liked traffic jams, and this was no exception.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>However, this traffic jam made me feel like I was living a normal life again. There were many scenes to look at. I was confused about which direction to take them all in.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>The street was lined with shops and markets. There were small amounts of clothes, vegetables, fruits, and even some meat.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Sellers were filling the place, whether calling out or standing. Many voices were calling, &ldquo;Come on! Many goods here. Gaza is living.&rdquo; Traders and sellers were arranging fruits and vegetables. There were mostly boxes of bananas and avocados.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>The smell of barbecue got all my attention. Its scent still lingers with me to this day.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>The blend of barbecue, fruits, and vegetables brought back bright memories. Many businesses lined the street. Many people of different ages were shopping, though only a few were buying, which meant everything was still too expensive and unaffordable.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Still, it was the nicest traffic jam I had ever been stuck in. The noise that I truly loved.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Gigantic pride and happiness washed me over. My city was starting to breathe again, and with each breath, it would give its people&mdash;who loved it most&mdash;something strong and kind.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I hoped the traffic jam would last a long time, so I could keep watching my city breathe and heal from pains far greater than the one in my ears.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Then the car continued its journey, and my eyes did not leave the windows. After 45 minutes, we were nearing our stop and finally reached it.</p>
</div>

<h2>Getting to the Clinic</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Abd Al-Rahman opened the car door, got out, followed by my sister and me. At that moment, the most wonderful feeling lit up every corner of my mind.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Even opening and closing the car door felt like something great I used to do almost daily before the war.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I was ready to continue my journey that had felt fantastic from the very beginning.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I whispered to Jasmine, &ldquo;Our university, which we haven&rsquo;t seen for two years, is around here.&rdquo;</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Jasmine said, &ldquo;How excited I am. We haven&rsquo;t seen it since the very beginning of the war.&rdquo;</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>We both looked to our right. The first scene I saw was a spread load of washed clothes on the nicest building on our university campus, the KSA building.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>My heart started aching. This was where I used to attend lectures and study as a human being. This is where I was meeting with lecturers, friends, and many new people. I sat in the wide green nature. I laughed and even cried at times. I was making use of every moment. I was honoured. I was living the nicest life.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p><strong>Now, nothing was the same.</strong></p>
</div>
