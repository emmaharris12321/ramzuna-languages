<div class="poem-block">
<p>I wished I was at home to have the luxury of crying. I miss my university as a place for studying, not as a shelter.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
Upon seeing this first scene, we decided not to enter the university. One reason was to keep the privacy of the families inside it and another was to avoid inflicting much pain on our hearts.
Hastily, Jasmine, Abd-Al-Rahman and I reached the doctor's office.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
Abd-Al-Rahman made sure that everything was ok, and we wouldn't have an obstacle; and he left us to do some things he aimed to.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
Jasmine and I sat waiting with a strange woman.
She was talkative and so was I, so not a single minute passed without us talking..</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Having the experience of talking with her as a strange woman made me feel like a human being finding a lost treasure. I love meeting new people when I am feeling lonely.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
Then, when Jasmine went ahead to watch our turns, I met a woman from our neighbourhood!
This was the biggest stroke of luck, the real treasure. We hugged and I immediately asked her about herself and her daughter "Donya", whom she had accompanied everywhere as her spoiled child.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
I opened my eyes wide and exclaimed, "Really!
Mashallah!" when she told me that Donya had started secondary school! She added, "The long war took much of our youth. Donya is no longer a child. She's helping me with everything and now taking care of the orphans at our tent until I can treat them."</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
She continued talking and talking. I felt she finally found someone to talk to.
She told me about her loss of everything. I felt sad for what she was going through, but I also felt consoled as we had similar fates.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
Time passed very quickly. I wished I could have sat more with that educated and polite woman, but my sister's turn and mine to meet the doctor came.</p>
</div>



<h2>Getting to the Doctor's Room</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
We entered the room by saying "Alsalam Alaykum".
Once we entered, 2 things caught my eye. The first thing was that the room was a real room made of SYONES!.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
I said to myself," Wow, there are still rooms made of stones in this part of the city that we are allowed to enter more than elsewhere!"</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
I stood beside the wall and touched it by my elbow.
It was a very strange touch, and I felt I wanted to touch it many times to believe it.
Meantime, I was naturally drawn to the air-conditioned atmosphere. It evoked good memories of our destroyed home! The contrast with the tent atmosphere was striking.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
In a few seconds, the doctor named me and Jasmine "Marah and Jasmine, how are you? How is your father? Have a seat please and welcome to our clinic."
Then, the doctor started treating Jasmine while I was watching.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
I curiously started mediating every single thing that was strange to see after a while. The tools that the doctor was using, the chairs that I was sitting on which were made of sponge, the chair for the sick person, the lamp, even the doctor's table deserved to be meditated on.
I meditated on when and how things would return to normal or even if they ever would.
Then it was my turn to be treated.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>
The doctor started talking with me and asking me some questions, and I was explaining to him. As it was the first time I had visited a doctor in ages, this was a new kind of luxury.
Thankfully, in 10 minutes, Jasmine and I were treated.</p>
</div>


<div class="poem-block">
<p>The doctor reassured us by saying that our case was an usual case, and that almost all people encounter similar situations. I am grateful for that doctor who made us feel at home.
Ultimately, we left the room after the doctor asked us to deliver his greetings to our father.</p>
</div>


<h2>Shopping on our Way to the House</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>On our way to the house we started shopping for some essentials Jasmine and I needed.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Walking in the market, I realised that all life was there.</p>
</div>
