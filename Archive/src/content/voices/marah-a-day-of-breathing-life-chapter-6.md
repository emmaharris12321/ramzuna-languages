<div class="poem-block">
<p>The city and its people cherished their lives like never before.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>At a moment, we reached a big attracting clothing store. We entered, and only one girl in her 20s was there. She was wearing a black suit and pink scarf.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Once we were there, she said, “Welcome, we are here to help you”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>While we were browsing the store, I kept thinking she wasn’t the only worker managing this big space.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>As she was very enthusiastic to talk with me, I was happy to talk with her. I asked her with pride, “Are you the only worker here?”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>She said, “Yes. I am the owner of this store!”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I showed her how proud I was of her, and reflected, “For ages I haven’t met successful people! I hope I will be successful one day”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>We purchased what we wanted, thanked her and left. Finally, the time to go back to the tent had arrived.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>While walking to the station, looking for a suitable car to ride, a man dashed towards us at the traffic jam.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>He asked, “Where are you going, sisters?”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>We answered him, and he led us to a car 2 minutes far away.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>We hopped in the car, and finally I was able to relax after that fantastic but exhausting journey.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>In the car, my mind was repeating some thoughts, “OMG, how alive my city feels! This was something unexpected and rewarding. I should be breathing now also or at least soon. I don’t really know if I am but I hope I am. I am feeling like a mess!”.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Still, I was trying to savour the happiness of the journey and make it last as long as I possibly could. I knew it would be a while before I could experience something new again.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Finally, we reached the nearest spot to our tent; the last allowed by the drivers.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Frankly, I felt a strong urge to return to where I had come. I wished I had remained there longer. I didn’t like the place we had been evacuated to.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Then, we started walking across the sandy terrain till we finally arrived after a 30-minute walk.</p>
</div>

<h2>Finally, at the Tent</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Once we reached the tent, I sat on my mattress fatigued, not able to make anything.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>My mother came to me, asking, “I heard the voices of Marah and Jasmine. Have they arrived?”</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>She had just baked something on the griddle as our oven had been destroyed as a result of a strong trough.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>When my mum realised my exhaustion, she explained that it was due to my prolonged lack of outdoor time. She assured me that a few outings would sort everything out.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Once my mum said that, the feeling of tiredness eventually turn into happiness. I had missed those vibes.Then, I went to sleep, to relax and recreate those pre-war feelings.</p>
</div>

<h2>The Next Day</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>On the second day, I opened my eyes and felt like heavy loads were upon me.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Throughout the night, many nightmares plagued me, all focused on my happiness the day before. This happiness was linked to my dreams of breathing freely. Sitting quietly, I wondered the cause of these disturbing dreams.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>The only reason I found was that I believed everyone else’s life was beautifully passing by while mine was not. I felt like I had severe disabilities.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>So, I started convincing myself that if the city was crowded like this, there would be many people inside their tents as it was my case. But, the nightmare kept chasing me.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Then, I started wondering if people were really living, or this was just what I wrongly realised.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>I kept remembering how people were thinking of me as a successful person, while in fact I didn’t feel the same. I yearned for success but didn’t know if I truly had it. I wasn’t sure if my life was truly enlightening.</p>
</div>

<h2>The Ongoing Question</h2>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Was I passive in thinking like this, or was it the most positive thing I could think of?</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Or, was I disrupted and this was the normal outcome of my situation?</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Amid deadly thoughts and nightmares, I keep reminding myself that goodness would prevail and come to everyone; nothing is ever permanent.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>One day, life would fully breathe again.</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Till then,</p>
</div>

<div class="poem-block">
<p>Good night!</p>
</div>

<h2>END</h2>
